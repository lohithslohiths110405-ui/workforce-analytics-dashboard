# Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Workforce Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Power BI. It analyzes employee attrition, workforce demographics, job roles, employee satisfaction, and retention trends to support data-driven HR decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition across different departments.
- Identify job roles with higher employee turnover.
- Understand the relationship between overtime and attrition.
- Analyze employee demographics and job satisfaction.
- Provide insights to improve employee retention.

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- MySQL
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV Dataset

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Workforce Analytics Dashboard
- Total Employees
- Attrition Count and Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Age
- Department-wise Attrition
- Job Role Analysis
- Gender and Business Travel Analysis
- Overtime and Education Field Analysis

### 2. Employee Insights Dashboard
- Attrition Rate vs Target
- Department-wise Attrition
- Attrition by Marital Status
- Employee Details Table
- Job Satisfaction Analysis
- Attrition by Job Level

## Key Insights
- Overall employee attrition rate: 16.12%.
- Total employees who left: 237.
- Research & Development has the highest attrition count.
- Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition among job roles.
- First-year employees show a significant attrition pattern.
- Overtime is an important factor to investigate in employee retention.
